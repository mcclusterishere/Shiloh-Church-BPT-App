Events & RSVPs

Draft events here (stored in this browser), then download events.json and commit it to publish — the app sorts future/past automatically. Each event shows its RSVP roster with one-tap email and text blasts.

Every visitor card, RSVP, prayer request, and reservation request POSTs to the webhook. Point it at Zapier, Make, n8n, or Resend and fan out to email, texts, or a CRM — full recipes in docs/BACKEND.md.
