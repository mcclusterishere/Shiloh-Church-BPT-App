THE <title> AND <meta description> LINES A FORKER REPLACES
===========================================================

These lines are fetched by the browser before any JavaScript runs, so the
app cannot re-theme them at runtime — they are what search engines,
bookmarks, and the install prompt see. Edit each one by hand after forking.
Line numbers are from the current tree (verify with the grep below if the
files have moved since):

    grep -n '<title>\|name="description"' index.html admin.html golive.html guide.html

index.html
----------
line 6:   <title>Shiloh Baptist Church</title>
          -> replace with:  <title>Your Church Name</title>

line 7:   <meta name="description" content="Shiloh Baptist Church, Bridgeport CT — Where Christ Is Lifted Up. Events, prayer, sermons, and connection.">
          -> replace with:  <meta name="description" content="Your Church Name, Your City — your tagline. Events, prayer, sermons, and connection.">

admin.html
----------
line 6:   <title>Back office · Shiloh Baptist Church</title>
          -> replace with:  <title>Back office · Your Church Name</title>

golive.html
-----------
line 6:   <title>Broadcast studio · Shiloh Baptist Church</title>
          -> replace with:  <title>Broadcast studio · Your Church Name</title>

guide.html
----------
line 6:   <title>The Shiloh App — Paper Guide</title>
          -> replace with:  <title>The Your-Church App — Paper Guide</title>

line 7:   <meta name="description" content="A large-print paper guide to the Shiloh Baptist Church app — how to put it on your phone and how to use it.">
          -> replace with:  <meta name="description" content="A large-print paper guide to the Your Church Name app — how to put it on your phone and how to use it.">

manifest.webmanifest
--------------------
lines 2-4 (name, short_name, description) are the same kind of
before-JavaScript metadata. template-pack/manifest.webmanifest already
carries the example-church values — replace them with your own church's.

The rest of the hardcoded church-name strings (hero eyebrow, onboarding
title, install instructions, and so on) are enumerated line-by-line in
docs/TEMPLATE.md step 8 — that map is the complete, honest list.
