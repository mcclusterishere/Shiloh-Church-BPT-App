How do I let the church know I'm coming to something?
Open Events at the bottom of the screen, find it, and tap Yes, Maybe, or No.
How do I share a prayer request?
Open Prayer at the bottom of the screen and fill in the short form. You choose who can see it.
What if I don't want everyone to see my request?
Choose "The prayer team only" or "The pastor only" when you share it. It will never show on the church-wide wall.
What if something isn't working?
Ask any deacon after service, or call the church office above. Nothing you tap can break anything.
