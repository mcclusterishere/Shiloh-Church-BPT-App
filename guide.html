Section 1

Putting the app on an iPhone

These steps are for an iPhone or iPad. If your phone is an Android, skip to Section 2. A family member or the church office can sit with you — it takes about a minute.

1 Open the app in Safari

Type the address from the front of this guide, or ask someone to open it for you.
2 Tap the Share button

It's the small square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen — this picture shows it.
3 Tap “Add to Home Screen”

Stroke the list upward slowly until you see it — it sits beside a square with a plus sign, like this one.
4 Tap “Add” in the top corner

That's the last tap — you're done.
5 Look at your home screen

The Shiloh picture now sits with your other apps. From now on, one tap on it opens the app.

Section 2

Putting the app on an Android phone

These steps are for Android phones using Chrome. If your phone is an iPhone, use Section 1 instead.

1 Open the app in Chrome

Type the address from the front of this guide, or ask someone to open it for you.
2 Tap the three little dots

They sit in the top corner of the screen, one above another — like this picture.
3 Tap “Add to Home screen”

Stroke the list upward slowly if you don't see it at first.
4 Tap “Add” or “Install”

Your phone may also offer an Install message at the bottom of the screen — tapping that works just as well.
5 Look at your home screen

The Shiloh picture now sits with your other apps. From now on, one tap on it opens the app.

Section 3

Using the app

Six buttons sit along the bottom of the app. Here is what the first five do. The sixth one, Help, can teach you all of this again — right on the phone.

Home — the picture of a house

The front page: service times, what's happening next, and a greeting from the pastor. If you ever feel lost, tap the house and you're back here.
Events — the little calendar

Everything coming up at church. Tap Yes on anything and the church will know to expect you.
Prayer — the small flame

Share a prayer request and pray along with the church family. You choose who sees yours: the whole church, the prayer team, or the pastor alone.
Groups — the two people

The ministries of the church. Find your place and tap “I'm interested” — the leader will reach out to you.
Give — the heart

Tithes and offering. Every button there goes straight to the church's own giving accounts — the app never touches your money.

Wherever you are in the app, stroke the screen upward slowly to read more — like smoothing a tablecloth.

Section 4
